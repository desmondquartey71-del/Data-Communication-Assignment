Five Key Sections

What I Studied

01

Satellite Relay

A communication satellite receives a signal from Earth, processes it, and sends it back toward another coverage area.

02

Radio and Microwave

Satellite links use radio and microwave frequencies so signals can travel through space between ground stations and satellites.

03

Transponders

Transponders amplify received signals and shift frequencies to reduce interference before retransmission.

04

Orbit Choice

Geostationary satellites appear fixed in the sky, while low Earth orbit satellites move quickly and work in constellations.

05

Uses and Reliability

Satellites support television, telephone, internet, radio, weather, emergency, and military communication services.
